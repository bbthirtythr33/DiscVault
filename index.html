How franchise ordering works

Enter a franchise name consistently (for example, “Star Wars”) to group its movies. Set each film’s release and chronological order numbers, then switch the sort menu. If you don’t know the in-universe order, leave it blank; those titles will appear after numbered entries.

Use Wikidata movie lookup to add a selected result directly to your collection. Cover art is searched automatically using Wikipedia's page thumbnails first, then Apple's free movie catalog. Some titles may not have matching artwork; those keep the film icon. Cover art needs an internet connection, but your collection remains saved in this browser.
